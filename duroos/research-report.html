RESEARCH & SELECTION · 4 OCTOBER 2026

Know what you are choosing.

50 book-based series and 1,118 indexed recordings across seven sciences. Arabic remains the main collection, with five clearly labelled English series by Abu Suhaib Bassam Ali.

Sources and individual lesson links have been inspected. Whole-book coverage, sound quality and every recording’s playback have not been audited. A surviving archive is not automatically a complete explanation.

New in this edition

Eighteen series and 382 recordings have been added: al-Albani’s Adab al-Mufrad recordings; Ibn Uthaymin on grammar, usul, tafsir and study etiquette; al-Shathri on usul; Haytham Sarhan on Hilyat Talib al-Ilm and Wasitiyyah; al-Khudayr’s Mimiyyah; Ibn Baz’s Rahbiyyah; al-Shuwayir’s Warqat; al-Ruhayli’s Four Principles; and Uthman al-Khamees’s fifty-hadith video explanations.

Abu Suhaib’s five English series cover Kitab al-Tawhid, Usul al-Sunnah, the Prophet’s Prayer Described, funeral rulings, and Tasfiyah wa Tarbiyah. The English additions were explicitly requested for this edition. Use the lesson-language filter to keep an Arabic-only view.

Al-Albani and Abu Suhaib

The al-Albani entry contains fourteen historical MP3 files whose names were inspected in Internet Archive’s file listing. A separate fourteen-video edition is linked as an alternative. Neither archive is treated as a modern microphone recording or certified as the whole of Adab al-Mufrad.

Abu Suhaib is identified as Bassam Ali in his own biography. His videos are linked individually from their observed playlists; a playlist’s presence does not establish whole-book completeness. The Prayer series includes forty explanations and a teaching demonstration.

How the indices were checked

Teacher attribution, book name, language, recording order, publisher and individual media links were inspected. The new YouTube lesson titles and durations were read from rendered playlists; durations are omitted when not observed. MP3 filenames were read from source links, not guessed. No recordings are rehosted.

One test attachment was excluded from Sarhan’s Wasitiyyah collection. Its 48 teaching and recorded question sessions reach the Companions section, but the full book’s ending is not established. Al-Shuwayir’s playlist exposes four parts; its separately verified fifth and final video is included directly.

The comparative microphone and modern video audit remains unfinished. No untested recording receives an “excellent sound” or “4K camera” badge. The original Four Principles presentation by al-Fawzan exposed a 2160p option, but uses slides over audio.

The original benchmark

The Rahmaniyyah prospectus supplied a map of traditional sciences and texts, without copying its timetable or institutional structure. Verification still required

Most sound quality is unassessed by listening. Compare modern official video editions; sample the beginning, middle and end of every series; match final-text coverage; collect actual recording durations; and recheck intermittent sources. Local server startup is blocked in this execution session; a local launcher and static upload folder are supplied. Playback depends on source availability. No online publication was requested.

Excluded or deferred

Al-Ruhayli’s 41-part Usul series has unresolved access failures; his seven Warqat lessons cover a section. The al-Fawzan Umdat source examined stops at the prayer chapter. Ibn Baz’s second Three Principles index did not establish coverage from the opening. A newer al-Badr Forty Hadith index did not resolve final coverage, so an older edition was used. English additions are explicitly labelled; unrelated talks are not used to inflate course counts.

Progress and content rights

The library links and plays recordings from their publishers. It does not rehost recordings or transcripts. Audio progress is saved locally; YouTube minutes and external lesson numbers are bookmarked manually. Export a progress backup to move devices. There is no account, cloud sync or automatic source monitoring.
