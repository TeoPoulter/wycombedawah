About Wycombe Da’wah

town centre.

For nearly twenty years, local Muslims have made time at the weekend to speak to their neighbours about Islam.

Visit the stall

High Wycombe town centre

Saturdays & Sundays

From Dhuhr (midday prayer), depending on setup.

Ask where we are this week

At the table

You can pick up a free English translation of the Qur’an, take a leaflet away or stay for a conversation. Some people have a question about what Muslims believe. Others just want to see what’s on the table.

If you’ve recently accepted Islam, you’re welcome to ask about prayer, purification or getting to know other Muslims locally. We can help you find introductory guides and someone to speak to.

Giving your time

Some of the brothers who volunteer today first met the team as visitors. If you’d like to help, read the policy and work through the WISE course. The Shura will arrange for you to shadow a senior brother and will tell you when you can speak to visitors yourself.
