FOUNDING CUSTOMER PACKAGE

Know what’s included.

£395 setup, followed by £19 per month for the first 12 months, for the first three customer madrasas. These are the proposed package details, not an online purchase or a binding service agreement. We confirm scope, total charges and hosting arrangements in writing before payment.

Setup

  • One branded portal for one institution, up to 150 active students and 10 staff.
  • Logo, colours, existing domain or subdomain connection, and standard registration questions.
  • Administrator, teacher, parent and student roles; classes and timetable configuration.
  • Attendance, homework and points workflows.
  • One student import using our spreadsheet template.
  • One 45-minute administrator training session and written instructions.
  • One review round before launch.

Monthly management

The proposed service includes hosting, routine updates, backups and email support, with up to 30 minutes of small configuration changes each month. We aim to respond within two working days; this is not a 24-hour support or guaranteed resolution service.

Backup frequency, retention, recovery arrangements and storage allowance will be specified in your service agreement before launch.

Work quoted separately

New modules, integrations, payment collection, WhatsApp automation, complex data clean-up, further imports and redesigns. Domains, paid third-party services and their usage charges are separate and require your agreement.

Delivery and cancellation

We target 10 working days from receipt of complete information, subject to an agreed start date and customer review. The founding monthly rate is fixed for your first year; any renewal rate will be agreed in advance.

We will agree cancellation, data export, deletion and any setup refund terms in writing before taking payment. There is no payment form on this website.

Technology and ownership

The portal is a customised Gibbon implementation. Gibbon is licensed under the GNU GPL; software notices and applicable source-code rights are preserved. Your institution’s records remain under your control. Madrasa Desk is an independent implementation service, not an official Gibbon product.
